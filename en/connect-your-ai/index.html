Guide · narelia-mcp

Connect Narelia to your AI.

Narelia for Mac comes with narelia-mcp, an MCP server your AI app starts on your own computer, over stdio. With it, the AI you already use reads your Narelia figures and can propose logs, which are only saved when you confirm them on the iPhone.

Before you start The steps The 22 apps If something’s off

Before you start

  • Narelia 1.1.21 or later on the iPhone, with Narelia Pro. “Connect with your AI” arrives with that version. Until it is out on the App Store, it is in the TestFlight beta.
  • Narelia for Mac. Download it here; it includes the window and narelia-mcp.
  • iCloud Drive on both, with the same Apple Account. It is how the encrypted file travels. Another Files folder your Mac syncs, such as Dropbox, also works.
  • An AI app with MCP. Any of the 22 on the list.

The steps

  1. On the Mac

    Download Narelia for Mac

    Move it to Applications. As it is not signed yet, the first time you open it with right-click → “Open” (how, step by step).

  2. On the iPhone

    Pair the iPhone: folder and password

    In Narelia, go to Settings → “Connect with your AI”. Read the conditions and tap “Accept and continue”. Tap “Use iCloud Drive › Narelia” and Narelia creates the folder. Then “Set password”: at least 8 characters. It encrypts the file before it leaves Narelia, and you will type the same one on the Mac. Keep it: Narelia cannot recover it.

    In “What your AI can see” you decide which categories are exported. You can change it or stop at any time.

  3. On the Mac

    Open Narelia: the folder and the same password

    The first time there are three steps. Narelia finds the folder by itself and you click “Use this folder”. Then you type the iPhone’s password; it is kept in the macOS Keychain, never in a file or in your apps’ configuration.

  4. On the Mac

    Pick your AI and click “Connect”

    Narelia adds its entry to that app’s configuration file, saving a copy next to it first (*.narelia-copia.*). Quit the AI app and open it again so it sees Narelia. For the seven by-hand apps, Narelia shows you the text, already with your folder, and where to paste it.

  5. In your AI

    Ask it

    How did I sleep this week compared with my usual?

    Compare my variability this month with last month.

    Log a coffee.

    Whatever your AI asks to log shows up on the iPhone under “Pending from your AI”, and is not saved until you confirm it. On the iPhone itself, keep using Siri to log: AI apps on a phone cannot reach a server on your computer.

The 22 apps

Fifteen are set up for you with “Connect”: Narelia writes its entry in the file shown. Seven are set up from their own window or terminal, and for those the exact text is here.

In the snippets, replace YOUR_USER with your Mac user name. The app gives them to you with your folder already filled in, under “Connect with your AI”. None of them contains the password.

    Set up for you

  • LM Studio

    Set up for you

  • AnythingLLM

    Set up for you

  • Jan

    By hand

    In Jan: Settings → MCP Servers → «+». Copy the command, arguments and the NARELIA_DIR variable.

    {
  "command": "/Applications/Narelia.app/Contents/MacOS/narelia-mcp",
  "args": [],
  "env": {
    "NARELIA_DIR": "/Users/YOUR_USER/Library/Mobile Documents/iCloud~com~diego~myhealth/Documents"
  }
}

If something’s off

Your AI doesn’t see Narelia
Quit the AI app completely and open it again: most read their configuration only at launch. In Claude Code, start a new session.
“I haven’t touched anything”
If the configuration file is not valid JSON, TOML or YAML, Narelia leaves it alone and tells you the line. Fix it and click “Connect” again. If your OpenCode uses opencode.jsonc, with comments, add the entry by hand under mcp.servers.
The Mac asks for Keychain access
That is narelia-mcp reading your password. While the app is unsigned, it can happen once per version: click “Always Allow”.
Removing the connection
On the Mac, “Remove” deletes only Narelia’s entry from that app. On the iPhone, “Stop and delete” deletes the files Narelia wrote in your folder and forgets the folder and password. What your AI already received stays with that AI.

Worth knowing

The file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and a key derived from your password (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds). In iCloud Drive, encryption cannot be turned off.

When you connect an AI, you are the one giving it that data; from then on that company handles it under its own terms, not Narelia’s. With a local model, as in LM Studio or Jan, it doesn’t leave your Mac.

Your AI is not Narelia: what it answers may be wrong, and it is not medical advice or a diagnosis. For any health question, ask a professional.
