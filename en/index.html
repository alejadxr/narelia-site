iPhone · Apple Watch · and now, Mac

Your data, measured against you.

Narelia reads your Apple Watch and compares you with yourself, on the iPhone and with no account. And now, from your Mac, it can bring your figures to the AI you already use. With no Narelia server in between, because there isn’t one.

Narelia for Mac

The iPhone app is free with no account, on iOS 26. Apple Watch recommended.

The path your figures take.

Four stops, all of them yours. What your AI proposes comes back the same way and waits on the iPhone until you say yes.

  1. Your iPhone

    Narelia works everything out here and writes only the figures you choose, encrypted with your password.

  2. Your folder

    iCloud Drive › Narelia, in your own account. Narelia creates it for you from the iPhone.

  3. Your Mac

    Narelia for Mac opens the file with the password kept in your Keychain, and serves it through narelia-mcp.

  4. Your AI

    Claude, Codex, Cursor, OpenClaw… The one you already use reads your figures and can propose a log.

Anything your AI asks to log goes back to the iPhone and is not saved until you confirm it there.

Narelia server There isn’t one: no account to create and no copy of anything of yours on our side.

The app

Every figure, against your own history.

Narelia does not compare you with a population chart. It compares you with your own nights and weeks, says how much each signal moved, and never claims more than the data supports.

  1. Your numbers against your own history

  2. Your stress through the day, the night kept apart

  3. Your effort against your own weeks

  4. Your vessels and your quick amounts

  5. No account, no server; iCloud only if you turn it on

Readiness
It starts from your morning recovery and moves with what the watch measures through the day. If you slept without the watch, it says so: “Daytime only”.
Recovery
Variability, resting heart rate, breathing and sleep against your own nights, with how much each signal moved.
Night, stress and vitals
The night by stage, stress through the day and each vital on its personal range.
Fitness
The week’s effort against your usual weeks, with your workouts and strength sessions.
Meals from a photo
Narelia suggests what is on the plate and you confirm it. It is read on the iPhone itself, with Apple Intelligence on compatible models.
Water and caffeine, in 3D
With your bottle, cup or can: drag the level on the vessel itself. Creatine and protein too.
Siri and Apple Intelligence
Ask for your variability, recovery or the night, and it answers like its screen does, without a network. Shortcuts offers “Get Narelia data”.
Watch and widgets
Apple Watch has its own app and complications; the iPhone, widgets in four styles.

The step-by-step guide

Beyond the app.

Readiness, hourly heart rate, stress, sleep, vitals, water and activity, on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch. In four styles: Narelia, Night, Editorial and Classic.

No account. No server.

All analysis happens on your iPhone. Your data lives there and in the Health app; the iCloud copy is optional, goes to your own account and is off until you turn it on.

With your AI, you choose which figures it sees and when it stops seeing them. The one receiving them is that AI, under its own terms: read them before you connect it.

No analytics and no advertising identifiers. Your own figures never sit behind a payment; Narelia Pro adds tools such as guided experiments, the PDF report and Connect your AI. Read the privacy policy.

Start with the iPhone.

Your baselines start forming with your first nights wearing the watch. The Mac and your AI, whenever you like.
